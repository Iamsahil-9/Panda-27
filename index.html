PANDA 27 · Privacy Information

This page explains how PANDA 27 handles information when you play the game.

1. Game Progress and Settings

PANDA 27 saves game information such as your progress, scores, selected animals, rewards and preferences. Some information is stored locally on your device.

2. Leaderboard

If online leaderboard functionality is enabled, information such as your player name, score, selected animal, distance, coins and stars may be sent to the game's configured server to display leaderboard results.

3. Advertisements

The game may display advertisements. Third-party advertising providers may process device or advertising-related information according to their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

4. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by services needed to operate features you use, such as online leaderboards and advertising. The actual information processed depends on the features enabled in your version of the game.

5. Children's Privacy

PANDA 27 should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, please contact the game support team.

6. Your Choices

You can manage available game preferences in Settings. Resetting game progress may delete locally stored game progress. It may not delete information already sent to an online service.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. Please review this page after important updates.

8. Contact Us

For privacy questions, open Help & Support from the Settings screen and use the available contact option.

Note: Before releasing the app, make sure this policy accurately describes your actual data collection, advertising SDKs, server practices and applicable legal requirements.
